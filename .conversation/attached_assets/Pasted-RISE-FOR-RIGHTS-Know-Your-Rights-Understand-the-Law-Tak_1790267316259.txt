RISE FOR RIGHTS

“Know Your Rights. Understand the Law. Take the Right Next Step.”

Build a high-level, production-quality, modern, trustworthy, mobile-first legal-rights platform for India called:

RISE FOR RIGHTS

The platform should feel like a real digital public-service product, not a traditional law-firm website and not a simple AI chatbot.

It should combine:

- modern banking-app simplicity
- Google Maps-style usability
- trustworthy government-service design
- accessible educational interfaces
- intelligent AI assistance
- verified legal information
- human legal-help pathways

The core philosophy is:

«“A trusted digital bridge between ordinary people and verified information about their rights, laws, government services and legitimate legal-help options.”»

Do NOT position the product as an “AI lawyer”.

---

1. IMPORTANT PRODUCT REQUIREMENT

Do NOT build only an MVP.

Build the website as a complete, polished, scalable product experience with all major screens, navigation, authentication, dashboards, legal library, AI assistant, accessibility features, professional mode, admin architecture and security architecture.

The legal database should start with a carefully curated set of high-impact legal topics, but the architecture must allow administrators to continuously add and verify additional laws later.

Do not attempt to include every Indian law.
